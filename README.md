# Wig Vibe Quiz

A fun, interactive quiz that helps Nigerian women discover their perfect wig style based on their mood, lifestyle, and personality.

## What It Does

Answer 6 quick questions about your vibe, occasion, maintenance preference, confidence mood, favorite hair expression, and budget. The quiz matches you with one of 7 wig styles:

- Lace Front Sleek Queen
- Bone Straight Diva
- Curly Bob Baddie
- Body Wave Goddess
- Braided Beauty
- Color Pop Queen
- Half Wig Natural

## Your Results Include

- Style match percentage
- Why this wig suits you
- Hair care tips
- Price range in Naira
- Confidence-boosting affirmation
